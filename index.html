2. Sensors Array

Multi-Parametric Sensing Matrix

High-precision micro-sensors selected for industrial resistance, low power draw, and high sample accuracy.

01 / BIOMETRIC

Heart Rate Sensor

Optical photoplethysmography (MAX30102) continuously measuring pulse rate and pulse ox (SpO2).

02 / BIOMETRIC

Blood Pressure Sensor

Estimated BP wave calculation via pulse transit time algorithm for detecting heat stroke / hypertension.

03 / MOTION

MPU6050 Accel/Gyro

6-axis Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) detecting sudden free-falls, cave-in impacts, and immobility.

04 / ACOUSTIC

MEMS Microphone Array

Samples environmental ambient decibels to drive ANC phase-inversion & acoustic protection.

05 / HAZARD

Gas Sensor Module

Detects Methane (CH4), Carbon Monoxide (CO), and toxic ambient gas spikes in deep mine shafts.

06 / LIGHTING

Ambient Light Sensor

GL5528 LDR sampling ambient lux to automatically dim or brighten the LED headlamp hands-free.

07 / POSITIONING

GPS & Indoor Beacon Module

NEO-6M GPS for surface tracking combined with UWB/BLE beacon triangulation for deep shaft positioning.

3. Core Technologies

Engineering & Hardware Stack

🧠

ESP32 Dual-Core Microcontroller

Acts as the central nerve hub running FreeRTOS. Handles multi-threaded sensor acquisition, DSP filtering for audio ANC, and protocol marshaling.

  • • 240MHz Tensilica LX6 Microprocessor
  • • Low Power Sleep Modes
  • • On-board ADC & DSP Engine
📻

LoRa Radio Communication

SX1276 LoRa transceiver operating at 868/915MHz. Penetrates solid subterranean rock barriers with minimal power consumption.

  • • Up to 5km Deep Range
  • • Sub-GHz Underground Waveguide
  • • Low Data Overhead Mesh
🔇

Active Noise Cancellation (ANC)

Real-time inverse wave audio generation (similar to high-end AirPods) to reduce heavy machinery noise while preserving human speech frequencies.

  • • 25dB Noise Reduction
  • • Adaptive Phase Inversion
  • • Emergency Bypass Mode

5. Execution Lifecycle

Step-by-Step Working Mechanism

1

Data Acquisition

Sensors continuously sample vitals (HR, BP), motion (IMU), gas concentrations, and acoustic levels.

2

On-Edge Processing (ESP32)

ESP32 runs local algorithms evaluating fall threshold equations, vital distress anomalies, and audio phase inversion.

3

Anomaly Detection & Trigger

If a fall or high heart-rate is detected, the system immediately switches to Alert Dispatch state.

4

LoRa Underground Telemetry Packet

An encrypted payload containing [Worker ID, Coordinates, Vitals Status, Gas Level] is transmitted via LoRa to the base station.

5

Active Noise Cancellation & Broadcast Switch

During normal operation, ANC stays ON. Upon emergency alert detection, ANC turns OFF instantly so siren tones & voice orders play loud and clear.
